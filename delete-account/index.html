Delete your Nippon2Go account

If you signed in to the Nippon2Go app with Google, you can delete your account and the data our server keeps here, without opening the app. In the app you can also do it in Profile → Account → Delete account.

With a Google account

Sign in with the same Google account you use in the app. We ask you to confirm before anything is deleted.

Delete your account?

Your Nippon2Go account data will be deleted and you’ll leave your shared trips. Your companions’ trips stay theirs, and your trips stay on your phones.

Signed in with Apple?

Delete the account in the iPhone app, in Profile → Account → Delete account: Apple will ask you to confirm. If you no longer have the app, install it again, sign in with Apple and delete it there. If you have any questions, write to carlos@nippon2go.com.

What is deleted

  • Straight away: your account, your first name, the link to your Google or Apple account, the copy of your trip saved to your account and the invitations you created. Your sessions stop working.
  • Shared trips: you leave all of them. What you added stays for the others, without your name, and your ticks on the group checklist are removed. If you organised a trip, the person who has been in it longest becomes the organiser; if nobody is left, the trip is deleted.
  • Within 30 days: the technical logs of requests to our server and the records of the changes you sent, which carry only a random account number.
  • Not deleted: the trips saved on your phones, which stay there until you delete the app.

More details in our privacy policy.
