Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The short version

  • You don't need an account to use Nippon2Go.
  • Your trips, routes and checklist are stored only on your iPhone. We cannot see them.
  • Our only server gives the app the key for Google’s place cards, passes Google place and hotel requests on to Google and, when you delete an account created with Sign in with Apple, asks Apple to revoke the app’s access. It also counts visits to our website’s download pages. It doesn’t keep your trips, your hotel searches or any Apple codes or tokens.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data. Our website loads a script from Travelpayouts, our affiliate network, and its download pages count visits without cookies; the one at nippon2go.com/app/ also uses Google Analytics, only if you accept (see section 4).
  • If you sign in, you can delete your account at any time in the app.
  • Some features use Apple, Google and Wikimedia services, which handle data under their own privacy policies.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the Nippon2Go iPhone app ("Nippon2Go", "we", "us"). If you have any questions about your privacy, contact us at carlos@nippon2go.com.

2. Data stored on your device

Nippon2Go saves the following only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage:

  • Your trip details: dates, daily plans, routes, saved places, and visit times and durations.
  • Your packing checklist, including any items you add.
  • Your preferences, such as app language and route pace.
  • A record of which plans you have added to your calendar, so they aren't added twice.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else, except the details described in section 4 (such as the Google identifier of a place or hotel saved in your trip, or your city and dates in a booking link you open). It is removed when you delete the app.

3. Optional sign-in

Signing in is optional, and the app works the same without it. Signing in does not create an account on any server of ours and does not upload your trips.

Sign in with Apple

If you sign in with Apple, Apple gives the app a user identifier and, the first time only, your name and email address. You can choose to hide your email. This information is stored only in your iPhone's secure Keychain and the app never sends it to us (see “Deleting your account” below for what our server receives from Apple when you delete your account). It is used to show that you are signed in. Because Apple shares your name and email only once, the app keeps them in the Keychain after you sign out, so it can show them again the next time you sign in with Apple. Like other Keychain data, they can stay on your iPhone after you delete the app, where only Nippon2Go can read them, unless you delete your account first (see below).

Sign in with Google

If you sign in with Google, Google's sign-in service gives the app your basic profile (name and email address). Google's software keeps your sign-in details in your iPhone's Keychain. We don't ask for access to any other Google services, such as Calendar or Drive. Google processes your sign-in under the Google Privacy Policy.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in Profile → Account → Delete account. If you signed in with Apple, Apple asks you to confirm and gives the app a one-time authorization code. The app sends that code to our server, which uses it with Apple to revoke Nippon2Go’s access to your Apple ID straight away. Apple’s reply to the server contains your Apple user identifier (and may contain your email address); the server uses it only to revoke the access and doesn’t keep or log the code, the tokens or anything in them. If you signed in with Google, the app asks Google to revoke its access. In both cases the app then removes everything it kept for the account on your iPhone, including the name and email kept in the Keychain. Your trips stay on your iPhone until you delete the app.

4. Services that connect to the internet

Google Places (place photos, details and hotel search)

To show place photos, the details of the hotel saved in your trip and hotel search results, the app sends requests to a small Nippon2Go server hosted on Google Cloud (region: Madrid, Spain), which passes them on to Google Maps Platform. These include the Google identifier of the place, or the hotel name you type and the city, plus your chosen language. The server doesn’t keep your trips or hotel searches. Google Cloud keeps standard technical logs of the requests our server receives (the time, your IP address, your device type and which Google place was requested) for up to 30 days, for security and to keep the service running. When the app starts, it also gets from our server the key that Google’s place details card needs, and keeps it only in memory. The place details card, the photos and their authors’ pictures are then loaded directly from Google, so Google also receives technical information such as your IP address and device type. Google may collect information about how its software is used. This is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Photos and place details are shown with Google's attributions and are not stored by the app.

Wikimedia Commons (some place photos)

When a place has no Google photo, or Google’s photo can’t be loaded, the app may show a photo loaded directly from Wikimedia Commons, so the Wikimedia Foundation receives technical information such as your IP address and device type. This is covered by the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy.

Account deletion with Apple

When you delete an account created with Sign in with Apple, the app sends Apple’s one-time authorization code to the same Nippon2Go server, which passes it to Apple to revoke the app’s access (see section 3). Google Cloud keeps the same standard technical logs of that request (the time, your IP address and your device type) for up to 30 days.

Apple Maps

Maps in the app are provided by Apple and are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Links to other services

Some buttons open other websites or apps, such as Google Maps (directions), Klook (activities and JR Pass), Airalo (eSIM), Skyscanner (flights and hotels), IATI (travel insurance) and Revolut (cards). Some links include details needed for your search, such as the destination, the city or your trip dates. Once you leave Nippon2Go, the other service's terms and privacy policy apply. We do not receive any information about what you do there.

Travelpayouts (this website only, not the app)

The nippon2go.com website loads a script from Travelpayouts, the affiliate network we work with, to confirm that we own the site and to manage affiliate links. When you open a page, your browser downloads the script from Travelpayouts’ servers, which receive technical data such as your IP address, your browser and the page you are visiting. The script can turn some links to travel services into affiliate links, so that Travelpayouts can record the click and, if you book, pay us a small commission at no extra cost to you. Travelpayouts may use cookies or similar technologies to do this. It handles this data under its own privacy policy. The Nippon2Go app does not include this script.

Visit counter and Google Analytics (this website’s download pages only, not the app)

On the download pages (nippon2go.com/app/ and nippon2go.com/go/, the link in our ads), the same Nippon2Go server counts each visit and each tap on the App Store and Google Play buttons. This uses no cookies and saves nothing on your device: your browser sends only which page it is (/app/ in Spanish or English, or /go/) and what happened (a visit, or which button was tapped). Google Cloud keeps the same standard technical logs of that request (the time, your IP address and your device type) for up to 30 days. We only look at the totals.

If you accept in the cookie notice on nippon2go.com/app/, it also loads Google Analytics, which uses cookies to show us how many people visit, roughly where from and on which kind of device, and how many tap a store button. Google signals and advertising features are turned off, and Google Analytics doesn’t log or store IP addresses. If you reject, Google Analytics isn’t loaded at all. You can change your answer at any time with the “Cookies” link at the bottom of that page; your answer is saved in your browser for a year. Google handles this data under the Google Privacy Policy (see also how Google uses information from sites that use its services). The Nippon2Go app doesn’t use Google Analytics.

5. Permissions

  • Calendar: only when you choose to add your itinerary to your calendar, Nippon2Go asks for permission to add events. It cannot read, change or delete your existing events.
  • Location: Nippon2Go does not access your device's location.

You can change permissions at any time in your iPhone's Settings.

6. What we don't do

  • The app doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Our website only loads the Travelpayouts script and, on the download pages, the visit counter and (on /app/, if you accept) Google Analytics described in section 4.
  • The app doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your personal data.
  • Our only server gives the app the key for Google’s place cards, passes Google place and hotel requests on to Google and, when you delete an account created with Sign in with Apple, asks Apple to revoke the app’s access. It also counts visits to our website’s download pages. It doesn’t keep your trips, your hotel searches or any Apple codes or tokens.

7. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and help you, and we delete it once it is no longer needed for that purpose. We do not add you to any mailing list.

8. Children

Nippon2Go is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Your rights

Because your trip data stays on your device, you are always in control of it: you can edit it in the app or remove it by deleting the app. To sign out, go to Profile → Account → Sign out. If you used Sign in with Apple, your name and email stay in your iPhone's Keychain after signing out (see section 3). To delete your account and everything the app kept for it, go to Profile → Account → Delete account (the option appears while you are signed in; if you have signed out, sign in again first). If you have contacted us by email, you can ask us to access, correct or delete that correspondence by writing to carlos@nippon2go.com. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Nippon2Go handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
