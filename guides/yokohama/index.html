Things to do in Yokohama

Yokohama, half an hour from Tokyo, is an open, easy-going port city: the towers and Ferris wheel of Minato Mirai, the Red Brick Warehouse, Japan's biggest Chinatown and Sankei-en Garden.

By Nippon2Go · Updated 25 November 2026

Many people see it on a day trip from Tokyo and, with time to spare, pair it with Kamakura, about 25 minutes away by train.

How many days?

One or two days. In one you see Minato Mirai and Chinatown; with two you add Yamashita, Yamate and Sankei-en Garden at an easy pace.

Yokohama day by day

Day 1 · Minato Mirai

  • Minato Mirai 21

    The waterfront district with Yokohama's towers, Ferris wheel and museums.

    Sight

  • Cup Noodles Museum

    An interactive museum about instant noodles, where you can decorate your own noodle cup.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Red Brick Warehouse

    Historic brick warehouses turned into shops, restaurants and an event space.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h

Day 2 · Chinatown, Yamashita and Sankei-en

  • Yokohama Chinatown

    Japan’s largest Chinatown, with restaurants, food stalls and shops.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Yamashita Park

    A waterfront park facing the port, with seaside walks, gardens and the moored Hikawa Maru ship.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Sankei-en Garden

    A traditional garden with ponds, a pagoda and historic buildings brought from other regions of Japan.

    Activity · Visit: about 1 h

More places in Yokohama in the app

  • Shin-Yokohama Ramen Museum

    A museum devoted to ramen, with a recreation of 1958 Tokyo and restaurants from different regions of Japan.

    Restaurant · Visit: about 1 h

  • Harbor View Park

    A hilltop park in Yamate with a rose garden and views of the port and the Bay Bridge.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Motomachi Shopping Street

    A shopping street at the foot of Yamate, with shops, cafés and boutiques.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h

  • Yokohama Kanteibyō Temple

    A brightly coloured Taoist temple dedicated to Guan Yu, in the heart of Chinatown.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Ōsanbashi Pier

    A cruise terminal with a public wooden and grass-covered deck overlooking the bay.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Yokohama Marine Tower

    An observation tower next to Yamashita Park with panoramic views of the port.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Nippon Maru Memorial Park

    A park with the Nippon Maru sailing ship and the Yokohama Port Museum.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Yokohama Air Cabin (Unga Park Station)

    An urban cable car crossing the harbour between Sakuragichō Station and Unga Park.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Yokohama Cosmo World

    An urban amusement park with a giant Ferris wheel, by the sea in Minato Mirai.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Tips

  • Minato Mirai lights up at dusk: the waterfront walk is the best part of the day.
  • At the Cup Noodles Museum you can decorate your own cup of noodles.
  • The Landmark Tower observation deck is closed for works (expected to reopen from 2028): for harbour views, try Marine Tower.

Getting there

  • Tokyo → Yokohama: JR train, about 30 min · about 530 yen
  • Yokohama → Kamakura: JR train, about 25 min · about 440 yen
  • Yokohama → Kyoto: Shinkansen, about 2 h · about 13,500 yen

Usual timetable times and fares for one adult, one way (reserved seat on the shinkansen), rounded. Check them before you travel.
