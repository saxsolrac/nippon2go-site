Things to do in Tokyo

Tokyo is huge, but it makes sense neighbourhood by neighbourhood: Asakusa and Sensō-ji temple, the Harajuku–Shibuya strip, Shinjuku with its viewpoints and nightlife, the bay with Odaiba and teamLab, and the markets of Ueno and Akihabara.

By Nippon2Go · Updated 25 November 2026

The trick is not to cross the city several times a day. That is why each day below groups places that are close to each other, the same way Nippon2Go builds your route. Tokyo is also the base for two day trips: Hakone and Mt Fuji.

How many days do you need in Tokyo?

Tokyo: For a first trip, 4 to 6 days. The first four days below cover the essentials; with 5 or 6 you add Ueno–Akihabara and Roppongi with Tokyo Tower. Hakone and Mt Fuji are separate day trips.

Nippon2Go splits your places into days at your pace: Relaxed (about 5 hours of visits and travel a day), Balanced (7) or Active (9). Visit times are the app's estimates.

Tokyo day by day

Day 1 · Asakusa and Skytree

  • Sensō-ji

    Tokyo's oldest Buddhist temple, entered through the Kaminarimon gate and its huge red lantern.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Asakusa

    Old-style district around Sensō-ji, with Nakamise street and its stalls.

    Sight

  • Tokyo Skytree

    Japan's tallest tower, with observation decks over the whole city.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Day 2 · Harajuku and Shibuya

  • Meiji Shrine

    A peaceful walk among giant cedar trees beside Harajuku.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Harajuku

    Takeshita Street and youth fashion, a short walk from Meiji Shrine.

    Sight

  • Shibuya Crossing

    The world's most famous pedestrian scramble, outside Shibuya Station.

    Sight

  • Shibuya Sky

    An open-air observation deck with one of Tokyo’s finest panoramas.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Day 3 · Tsukiji, teamLab and Odaiba

  • Tsukiji Outer Market

    Stalls selling seafood, tamagoyaki and Japanese knives; best in the morning.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h

  • teamLab Planets TOKYO

    An immersive digital art museum in Toyosu that you walk through barefoot, with rooms of knee-deep water, a garden of hanging orchids and a moss garden; booking a time slot is advisable.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Odaiba

    Man-made island on the bay, with a waterfront promenade and Rainbow Bridge views.

    Sight

Day 4 · Shinjuku

  • Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden

    A 58-hectare national garden a few minutes from Shinjuku Station, with Japanese, English landscape and French formal gardens and about 900 cherry trees; entry costs 500 yen.

    Activity · Visit: about 1 h

  • Tokyo Metropolitan Government Observatory

    Free observation deck on the 45th floor of the Metropolitan Government Building in Nishi-Shinjuku.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Shinjuku Golden-Gai

    Alleys packed with over 200 tiny bars; it comes alive at night.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Day 5 · Ueno and Akihabara

  • Ameyoko Market

    Street market along the train tracks in Ueno, with food stalls and shops.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h

  • Akihabara Electric Town

    Tokyo's district for electronics, anime and video games.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h

Day 6 · Roppongi and Tokyo Tower

  • Roppongi

    Neighbourhood of art museums and nightlife, home to Roppongi Hills.

    Sight

  • Tokyo Tower

    The red-and-white tower from 1958, with views over central Tokyo.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Day 7 · Day trip to Hakone

  • Owakudani

    Hakone's volcanic valley, with steam vents and the famous black eggs boiled in its sulphur springs.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Hakone Shrine

    Shrine on the shore of Lake Ashi, with its torii standing at the water's edge.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

Day 8 · Day trip to Mt Fuji

  • Arakurayama Sengen Park

    The park of Chureito Pagoda, with the classic view of Mt Fuji behind it.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

More places in Tokyo in the app

  • Ichiran Ramen Shibuya

    Customisable tonkotsu ramen in a distinctly Japanese dining experience.

    Restaurant · Visit: about 1 h

  • Sumida Aquarium

    An aquarium on the 5th and 6th floors of Tokyo Skytree Town, with one of Japan’s largest open indoor pools for its Magellanic penguins and a jellyfish zone; it pairs well with the Skytree.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Tokyo Disneyland

    Disney’s theme park in Urayasu (Chiba), about 15 minutes by train from Tokyo Station to Maihama on the JR Keiyo Line; it’s best to buy tickets in advance.

    Activity · Visit: about 8 h

  • Warner Bros. Studio Tour Tokyo (Harry Potter)

    A self-paced tour of the sets, costumes and creatures from the Harry Potter and Fantastic Beasts films, a 2-minute walk from Toshimaen Station (20 minutes from Shinjuku on the Oedo Line); allow 3 to 4 hours, and tickets are for a set date and time, best booked in advance.

    Activity · Visit: about 4 h

  • Tokyo Sea Life Park

    An aquarium in Kasai Rinkai Park on Tokyo Bay, entered through a glass dome, with a large ring-shaped tank of bluefin tuna; entry costs 700 yen and it closes on Wednesdays.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Tokyu Kabukicho Tower

    Entertainment tower in Kabukicho, in the heart of Shinjuku.

    Activity · Visit: about 1 h

  • Menchirashi

    Udon restaurant in Jingumae, near Harajuku.

    Restaurant · Visit: about 1 h

  • Iyashinosato (historic Japanese village)

    Open-air museum of thatched-roof houses by Lake Saiko, in the Mt Fuji area.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • JAPANKART Shinjuku (go-karting in Tokyo)

    Guided 90-minute go-kart ride through Shinjuku, Omotesando and Shibuya; you need a driving licence and an international permit.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Tips

  • Go to Asakusa early: Sensō-ji is free and much quieter first thing in the morning.
  • Book a time slot in advance for Shibuya Sky and teamLab Planets.
  • An IC card (Suica or Pasmo, also on your phone) works on the metro and JR trains.

Getting there

  • Tokyo → Kyoto: Shinkansen, about 2 h 15 min · about 14,000 yen
  • Tokyo → Osaka: Shinkansen, about 2 h 30 min · about 14,700 yen
  • Tokyo → Yokohama: JR train, about 30 min · about 530 yen
  • Tokyo → Kamakura: JR train, about 1 h · about 1,050 yen
  • Tokyo → Hakone: Romancecar, about 1 h 25 min · about 2,500 yen
  • Tokyo → Kawaguchiko (Mt Fuji): Bus or train, about 2 h · about 2,200 yen

Usual timetable times and fares for one adult, one way (reserved seat on the shinkansen), rounded. Check them before you travel.
