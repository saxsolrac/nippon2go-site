Japan in 10 days: Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka

This is the classic first trip and the one Nippon2Go suggests by default: with 10 days it gives 4 to Tokyo, 3 to Kyoto and 3 to Osaka, and fills each day with places close to each other.

By Nippon2Go · Updated 25 November 2026

Below are the 10 days with what to see on each and the trains between cities. Nara fits as a day trip from Kyoto or Osaka if you swap one of their days.

How many days in each city?

Nippon2Go splits the 10 days like this: 4 in Tokyo, 3 in Kyoto and 3 in Osaka.

Nippon2Go splits your places into days at your pace: Relaxed (about 5 hours of visits and travel a day), Balanced (7) or Active (9). Visit times are the app's estimates.

If you save your flights in the app, the first day starts 90 minutes after landing and the last one ends 3 hours before take-off.

Day 1 · Tokyo · Asakusa and Skytree

  • Sensō-ji

    Tokyo's oldest Buddhist temple, entered through the Kaminarimon gate and its huge red lantern.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Asakusa

    Old-style district around Sensō-ji, with Nakamise street and its stalls.

    Sight

  • Tokyo Skytree

    Japan's tallest tower, with observation decks over the whole city.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Things to do in Tokyo →

Day 2 · Tokyo · Harajuku and Shibuya

  • Meiji Shrine

    A peaceful walk among giant cedar trees beside Harajuku.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Harajuku

    Takeshita Street and youth fashion, a short walk from Meiji Shrine.

    Sight

  • Shibuya Crossing

    The world's most famous pedestrian scramble, outside Shibuya Station.

    Sight

  • Shibuya Sky

    An open-air observation deck with one of Tokyo’s finest panoramas.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Day 3 · Tokyo · Tsukiji, teamLab and Odaiba

  • Tsukiji Outer Market

    Stalls selling seafood, tamagoyaki and Japanese knives; best in the morning.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h

  • teamLab Planets TOKYO

    An immersive digital art museum in Toyosu that you walk through barefoot, with rooms of knee-deep water, a garden of hanging orchids and a moss garden; booking a time slot is advisable.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Odaiba

    Man-made island on the bay, with a waterfront promenade and Rainbow Bridge views.

    Sight

Day 4 · Tokyo · Shinjuku

  • Shinjuku Gyoen National Garden

    A 58-hectare national garden a few minutes from Shinjuku Station, with Japanese, English landscape and French formal gardens and about 900 cherry trees; entry costs 500 yen.

    Activity · Visit: about 1 h

  • Tokyo Metropolitan Government Observatory

    Free observation deck on the 45th floor of the Metropolitan Government Building in Nishi-Shinjuku.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Shinjuku Golden-Gai

    Alleys packed with over 200 tiny bars; it comes alive at night.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Day 5 · Kyoto · Fushimi Inari and Higashiyama

🚄 Travel Tokyo → Kyoto: Shinkansen, about 2 h 15 min (about 14,000 yen).

  • Fushimi Inari Taisha

    Thousands of red torii gates form one of Japan’s most iconic trails.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Kiyomizu-dera

    A historic temple overlooking the narrow streets of Higashiyama.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Sannenzaka

    A street in Higashiyama with shops and traditional buildings, near Kiyomizu-dera.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h

Things to do in Kyoto →

Day 6 · Kyoto · Arashiyama and the Golden Pavilion

  • Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

    A path among tall bamboo in Arashiyama, west of Kyoto.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion)

    The Golden Pavilion, covered in gold leaf and mirrored in its pond.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

Day 7 · Kyoto · Nishiki, Gion and Shirakawa

  • Nishiki Market

    Local food stops for sampling sweets, fish and pickles.

    Restaurant · Visit: about 1 h

  • Gion

    A neighbourhood in Higashiyama for strolling through the streets and discovering Kyoto’s traditional atmosphere.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Shirakawa Canal

    A walk beside the Shirakawa Canal in the Gion area.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

Day 8 · Osaka · The castle and Dotonbori

🚄 Travel Kyoto → Osaka: JR train, about 30 min (about 600 yen).

  • Osaka Castle

    Osaka's rebuilt castle, with a museum inside the keep and a large park around it.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Dotonbori

    Neon lights, canals and nightlife in the heart of Osaka.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

Things to do in Osaka →

Day 9 · Osaka · Kuromon and Shinsekai

  • Kuromon Ichiba Market

    A food market perfect for takoyaki, sushi and seafood.

    Restaurant · Visit: about 1 h

  • Shinsekai Hondori Shopping Street

    A shopping stroll through Shinsekai, near Tsūtenkaku.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h

  • Tsūtenkaku

    A tower and observation deck in Shinsekai for seeing Osaka from above.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Day 10 · Osaka · Umeda and the aquarium

  • Umeda Sky Building (osaka)

    Two towers joined at the top by an open-air circular observatory.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan

    An aquarium on Osaka Bay, in Tempozan, explored on a downward spiral around the great 9-metre-deep Pacific Ocean tank, home to whale sharks.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h
