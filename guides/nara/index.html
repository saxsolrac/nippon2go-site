Nara in one day

Nara was Japan's first permanent capital, in the 8th century, and its highlights sit in a single park: more than a thousand free-roaming deer, Tōdai-ji with its bronze Buddha of about 15 metres, and Nara National Museum.

By Nippon2Go · Updated 25 November 2026

It is all walkable and fits easily in one day, which is why almost everyone visits on a day trip from Kyoto or Osaka.

How many days?

One day is enough. It is about 45 minutes by train from Kyoto and about 40 from Osaka.

Nippon2Go splits your places into days at your pace: Relaxed (about 5 hours of visits and travel a day), Balanced (7) or Active (9). Visit times are the app's estimates.

Nara day by day

Day 1 · Nara Park, Tōdai-ji and the museum

  • Nara Park

    Walk among deer and visit monumental temples on a day trip.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Tōdai-ji

    An 8th-century Buddhist temple and World Heritage Site whose Great Buddha Hall, one of the largest wooden buildings in the world, houses a bronze Buddha about 15 metres tall.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Nara National Museum

    A museum of Buddhist art opened in 1895 in Nara Park, with sculptures from the Asuka to the Kamakura periods and, every autumn, the exhibition of the Shōsō-in treasures.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

Tips

  • Deer crackers (shika senbei) are sold in the park: don't show them all at once, the deer push.
  • Arrive early to see the Great Buddha Hall with fewer people.

Getting there

  • Kyoto → Nara: Train, about 45 min · about 750 yen
  • Osaka → Nara: Train, about 40 min · about 700 yen
