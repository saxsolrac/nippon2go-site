Things to do in Osaka

Osaka is Japan's food city: takoyaki, okonomiyaki and kushikatsu under the neon of Dotonbori, Kuromon market and retro Shinsekai. It also has the castle, one of Japan's biggest aquariums and Universal Studios Japan.

By Nippon2Go · Updated 25 November 2026

It makes a good base, too: Kyoto is about 30 minutes away by train and Nara about 40, so many travellers stay here and take day trips.

How many days?

2 or 3 days for the city, plus one full day if you go to Universal Studios Japan.

Nippon2Go splits your places into days at your pace: Relaxed (about 5 hours of visits and travel a day), Balanced (7) or Active (9). Visit times are the app's estimates.

Osaka day by day

Day 1 · The castle and Dotonbori

  • Osaka Castle

    Osaka's rebuilt castle, with a museum inside the keep and a large park around it.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Dotonbori

    Neon lights, canals and nightlife in the heart of Osaka.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

Day 2 · Kuromon and Shinsekai

  • Kuromon Ichiba Market

    A food market perfect for takoyaki, sushi and seafood.

    Restaurant · Visit: about 1 h

  • Shinsekai Hondori Shopping Street

    A shopping stroll through Shinsekai, near Tsūtenkaku.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h

  • Tsūtenkaku

    A tower and observation deck in Shinsekai for seeing Osaka from above.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Day 3 · Umeda and the aquarium

  • Umeda Sky Building (osaka)

    Two towers joined at the top by an open-air circular observatory.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

  • Osaka Aquarium Kaiyukan

    An aquarium on Osaka Bay, in Tempozan, explored on a downward spiral around the great 9-metre-deep Pacific Ocean tank, home to whale sharks.

    Activity · Visit: about 2 h

Day 4 · Universal Studios Japan

  • Universal Studios Japan

    A theme park in Sakurajima for a day of attractions during your trip.

    Activity · Visit: about 8 h

Day 5 · Shitennō-ji and Tenjinbashi-suji

  • Shitennō-ji

    One of Japan's oldest temples, founded in the 6th century.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

  • Tenjinbashi-suji Shopping Street

    Japan's longest covered shopping street, about 2.6 km long.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h

More places in Osaka in the app

  • Namba Yasaka Shrine

    Shrine whose stage is shaped like a giant lion's head.

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h 30 min

    Sight · Visit: about 1 h

  • Nakanoya

    A sushi restaurant in the Kyobashi area, on floor B1.

    Restaurant · Visit: about 1 h

  • 512 CAFE& America-mura

    Café in America-mura for a break between the shops of Shinsaibashi.

    Restaurant · Visit: about 1 h

Tips

  • Dotonbori is at its best after dark, with every sign lit up.
  • Buy Universal Studios tickets in advance, especially during Japanese holidays.
  • Kuromon is a morning and lunchtime market: many stalls close in the afternoon.

Getting there

  • Tokyo → Osaka: Shinkansen, about 2 h 30 min · about 14,700 yen
  • Kyoto → Osaka: JR train, about 30 min · about 600 yen
  • Osaka → Nara: Train, about 40 min · about 700 yen
  • Osaka → Hiroshima: Shinkansen, about 1 h 25 min · about 11,000 yen

Usual timetable times and fares for one adult, one way (reserved seat on the shinkansen), rounded. Check them before you travel.
